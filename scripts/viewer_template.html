How to read these resultsThese are held-out-golfer research predictions, using the whole swing including follow-through. Video + watch averages about 30 yards absolute error across 72 labeled shots. The assisted ball pilot averages about 25 yards across 31 shots and requires manually reviewed points. These averages are not confidence intervals. The under-100-yard flag is a short-shot proxy, not a diagnosis of chunked, thin or hosel contact. Recorded outcomes are displayed for comparison and are not prediction inputs.
