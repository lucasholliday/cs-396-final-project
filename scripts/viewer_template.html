How to read these results & sensing modalities

The purple ball uses predicted carry plus an estimate of forward rollout from six predicted flight measurements. The model separately estimates the sideways position at landing and at rest. See the performance summary for errors on held-out golfers.

Ground Truth (Cyan Arc & Ball): TrackMan Doppler radar recorded carry, launch angle, ball speed, launch direction, and aerodynamic curvature. Overlaid here strictly as the ground truth benchmark for direct visual and quantitative comparison.

Abstention Policy (Unverified Ball Tracking): If the autonomous camera tracker cannot identify a confident departure ray (e.g., severe tee glare), the model abstains from making a prediction rather than falling back to uncalibrated models, and excludes the shot from accuracy statistics.

Full Multi-Modal Architecture & Benchmark


Multi-Modal Sensing Architecture & Performance Summary



Comprehensive Leave-One-Golfer-Out (LOGO) cross-validation evaluation across all 70 camera-tracked strikes. Unseen golfers are evaluated with zero player identity leakage and zero radar inference inputs.








  

    📹
    High-Speed Camera (240 fps)
  

  

    Extracts autonomous early ball departure velocity vectors, vertical launch elevation angle, and pre-impact optical flow clubhead delivery speed.
  

  

    Primary Targets: Flat Carry (15.77 yd MAE), Launch Angle (2.58°), Ball Speed (8.61 mph), Launch Direction (4.23°).
  






  

    ⚡
    Clubhead Gyroscope (188.6 Hz)
  

  

    Unsaturated 3D angular rate ($g_x, g_y, g_z$) captures dynamic clubface orientation, impact closure rate ($d\text{face}/dt$).
  

  

    Primary Targets: Lateral Curve / Face-to-Path (21.53 ft MAE, 13.88 ft MedAE; 28.7% error reduction).
  






  

    🎯
    Visual-Inertial Fusion
  

  

    Couples camera horizontal launch ray with gyro impact curvature to model aerodynamic Magnus deflection and 2D ground rollout.
  

  

    Primary Targets: Carry Side Landing (37.09 ft MAE), 2D Landing Error (24.28 yd MAE vs 38.59 yd baseline).
  






  

    ⌚
    Apple Watch IMU & Pose
  

  

    Detects high-g wrist acceleration shock for contact window localization, downswing transition tempo, and MediaPipe hand anchoring.
  

  

    Primary Targets: Temporal sync < 8 ms, swing phase segmentation, 3D anatomical avatar IK.

Primary Launch Model vs. Fallback Model Breakdown

    

    

      Abstention Principle: Accurate when confident; transparent when uncertain.
    

  

  
  

    A golfer would much rather have the launch monitor be laser-accurate when confident and explicitly report "Didn't get a good read" when the camera misses the ball or the track is too short, rather than confidently asserting an ungrounded cohort average. In our Leave-One-Golfer-Out evaluation, 14 out of 72 swings lacked sufficient early frames for perspective geometry and fell back to predicting the training-set mean (~98–104 yd). Isolating the confident swings reveals the true physical accuracy of the 2-feature launch model:
