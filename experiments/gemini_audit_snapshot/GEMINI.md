## 1. Executive Summary & Mission

The goal of this project is to predict and visualize full golf shot trajectories and outcomes using consumer-grade/wearable and vision sensors (high-speed camera at 240 fps, Apple Watch IMU, clubhead IMU) as a low-cost alternative to commercial Doppler radar launch monitors ($20,000+ TrackMan 4).

TrackMan radar labels (`trackman_shot_labels.xlsx`) serve strictly as ground-truth training/evaluation targets and benchmark comparison, **never as runtime inference inputs**. All predictions are evaluated strictly under **Leave-One-Golfer-Out (LOGO)** cross-validation to prevent identity leakage across players.

---

## 2. Core Sensing Modalities & Dataset

1. **High-Speed Camera Video (`Video Data/`)**:
   - 240 fps, $1080\times1920$ portrait, down-the-line / oblique angle.
   - Captures pre-impact clubhead delivery and post-impact early ball launch (first ~15–30 ms).
   - Raw folders: `lucas_1`, `lucas_2`, `sid_1`, `sid_2`, `edward_1`, `edward_2`.
2. **Apple Watch IMU (`Watch Data/`)**:
   - High-rate accelerometer and gyroscope data capturing wrist kinematics and swing phase transitions.
3. **Clubhead IMU (`Club Data/`)**:
   - Sensor affixed near the hosel/grip. Note: Raw clubhead accelerometer experiences high-g saturation at impact; initial linear models exclude clipped acceleration in favor of rotational velocity and visual tracking.
4. **TrackMan Ground Truth (`trackman_shot_labels.xlsx`)**:
   - Flat Carry (yd), Total Distance (yd), Ball Speed (mph), Clubhead Speed (mph), Launch Angle (deg), Launch Direction (deg), Spin Rate (rpm), Apex Height (ft), Curve (ft), Carry Side (ft).

> **CRITICAL RULE**: Raw data directories (`Club Data/`, `Video Data/`, `Watch Data/`, `trackman_shot_labels.xlsx`) are strictly **immutable**. Never modify or overwrite them.

## 3. Key Systems & Features Built

### A. Video Ball Tracking & Pre-Impact Clubhead Velocity
- **Ball vs. Club Disambiguation**: Resolved severe tracking failures where visual trackers confused the shiny iron clubhead with the white ball during release (specifically fixing `lucas_1_02`, `lucas_1_09`, `lucas_1_10`, `lucas_1_12`).
- **Pipeline (`scripts/video_launch_tracker.py`, `scripts/separate_ball_club.py`)**:
  - Implemented high-pass temporal differencing and dual-polarity silhouette extraction.
  - Directional spatial gating: Separated downward/forward club path from upward/forward ball departure cone ($+10^\circ \text{ to } +35^\circ$).
  - Extracted authentic early ball launch velocity vectors and launch elevation angles.
  - Optical flow pre-impact clubhead speed estimation (`scripts/native_club_flow.py`) over the final 8 frames prior to impact.

### B. Autonomous Video Launch Prediction Models (TrackMan Replacement)
- **Model Script**: `scripts/video_launch_benchmark.py`, results in `reports/video_launch_tracking_results.md`.
- **Methodology**: Leave-One-Golfer-Out (LOGO) cross-validation evaluating Ridge, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosted regressors.
- **Inputs at Inference**: Autonomous visual ball velocity vector, climb angle, and pre-impact clubhead speed (zero radar inputs).
- **Performance**:
  - **Carry Distance**: **26.04 yd MAE** across all unseen golfers (beating multi-modal baseline of 35.88 yd MAE).
  - **Launch Direction**: **4.50° MAE** across unseen golfers.
  - Validated by unit tests in `tests/test_video_launch_tracking.py`.

### C. Aerodynamic Flight Continuation Engine
- **Engine Script**: `scripts/ball_continuation.py`.
- **Aerodynamics**: Implemented realistic golf ball flight mechanics with gravitational acceleration ($9.81\,\text{m/s}^2$), quadratic drag ($C_D \approx 0.22 - 0.28$), and Magnus lift ($C_L \approx 0.16 - 0.22$) generated by backspin.
- **Hang Time Calibration**: Fixed unrealistic quick-drop bug (previously falling in <1.0s) by extending simulation hang times to authentic 5.5s – 7.0s intervals, allowing the ball to apex downrange smoothly.
- **Autonomous vs. Benchmark Mode**: In autonomous mode, hang time and apex are inferred directly from camera launch rise and initial velocity.

### D. Anatomical 3D Golfer Avatar & Inverse Kinematics
- **Scene Script**: `scripts/range_scene.js`.
- **Anatomical Inverse Kinematics (`solveKnee`)**:
  - Replaced noisy MediaPipe landmark jitter with analytical two-bone IK using standard anthropometric femur (0.42m) and tibia (0.40m) lengths.
  - Constrained knee flexion strictly to the forward sagittal plane aligned with pelvis rotation. Completely eliminated backward knee hyperextension ("flamingo legs") and flickering limb lengths while preserving smooth, authentic hip rotation and lead-leg posting at impact.
- **Realistic Forged Iron**:
  - Modeled a stepped chrome steel shaft, Tour Velvet black rubber grip with white cap ring, black hosel ferrule, and a sculpted forged muscle-back blade with a satin face plate featuring authentic $34^\circ$ 7-iron loft and grooves.
  - Displayed in the golfer's hands across all 76 shots.
- **Ball on Tee Mat**:
  - Positioned the golf ball squarely on the synthetic driving range hitting mat atop a dedicated rubber tee spot at $(X = 0.48\,\text{m}, Y = 0.068\,\text{m}, Z = -0.05\,\text{m})$.
  - Ball rests on the mat until the club strikes it at impact.

### E. Interactive 3D Driving Range & Dual Comparison Visualizer
- **Single Elevated TrackMan Perspective**:
  - Fixed camera at $(X = 2.0\,\text{m}, Y = 3.6\,\text{m}, Z = 6.4\,\text{m})$ pitched down toward $(X = -0.1\,\text{m}, Y = 0.6\,\text{m}, Z = -60.0\,\text{m})$.
  - Eliminates horizon perspective flattening, giving an unobstructed view of the downrange landing zone to clearly compare where both balls land while keeping the golfer and mat visible in the lower foreground.
  - Eliminated distracting camera jumping/blending during playback.
- **Simultaneous Dual 3D Flight Arcs**:
  - **TrackMan Radar Truth** (cyan `#38bdf8`): Built from Doppler radar labels (`Carry flat (yd)`, `Launch direction (deg)`, `Curve signed (ft)`, `Carry side (ft)`, `Height (ft)`).
  - **Best Video Launch Model** (purple `#c084fc`): Built from autonomous LOGO predictions (`Carry flat (yd)`, `Launch direction (deg)`, apex height).
- **Thick 3D Tracer Ribbons & Landing Rings**:
  - Dynamic 8 cm wide quad-strip 3D tracer ribbons (`ribbonTruth` and `ribbonPred`) with glowing Toptracer-style trails in WebGL.
  - Dual landing bullseye rings on the turf that illuminate at touchdown ($progress \ge 0.95$), marking the exact landing coordinates.
- **Live Floating Yardage Badges**:
  - HTML badges floating directly above each flying ball in 3D screen space, ticking up in real-time from `0 yd` as the ball flies, then stopping and settling at final carry distance upon touchdown.
- **Natural Driving Range Environment**:
  - Thick 26 cm white target centerline with cross hash marks at 50, 100, 150, 200, and 250 yd.
  - Six scattered target greens at realistic varied locations with color-coded flags: 50 yd left (red), 90 yd right (yellow), 125 yd center-left (white), 165 yd right (blue), 210 yd left (red), 250 yd deep (gold).
  - Four organic golden-sand practice bunkers (`#dfce9e`) with raised turf lips along the fairway at depths of 80 yd, 138 yd, 167 yd, and 214 yd.

## 5. How to Run, Test, and Rebuild

1. **Run Unit Tests**:
   ```bash
   .venv/bin/python -m unittest discover -s tests -v
   ```
   *(All 29 tests must pass)*

2. **Verify 3D Range Scene Geometry**:
   ```bash
   node scripts/verify_range_scene.js
   ```
   *(Checks all 76 shots and 7,980 frames for finite coordinates, visible limbs, and grip alignment)*

3. **Rebuild the Research Viewer**:
   ```bash
   .venv/bin/python scripts/build_research_viewer.py
   ```
   *(Regenerates `outputs/research_viewer/index.html`)*

4. **Inspect in Browser**:
   Open [Open Swing Lab.html](file:///Users/lucasholliday/Downloads/CS%20396%20ML%20%2B%20Sensing/Final%20Project/Open%20Swing%20Lab.html) directly in Google Chrome or default browser.
